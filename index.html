How to Play

Moving

Use the arrow pad (or joystick) at the bottom left to walk. Double-tap forward to sprint. Drag anywhere on the right side of the screen to look around.

Jumping, Sneaking & Flying

Tap the jump button to jump. The middle of the arrow pad toggles sneaking. In Creative, double-tap jump to fly, then use the up/down buttons.

Mining & Building

Press and hold on a block to break it. Tap to place the selected block. Tap a TNT block to light it.

Inventory & Crafting

Tap a hotbar slot to select it, and tap the ••• button to open your inventory. In Survival the crafting list shows what you can make from your items.

Survival

Watch your health and hunger. Eat apples (sometimes dropped by leaves) or pumpkin pie by selecting them and tapping. Avoid long falls!

Keyboard

WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to mine, right click to place, Space to jump, Shift to sneak, E for inventory, T for chat, 1–9 for hotbar, Esc to pause.
